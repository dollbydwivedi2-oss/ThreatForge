🎣 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyberattack where criminals impersonate trusted entities — banks, companies, or people you know — to trick you into revealing sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data.

  • Fake emails that look like they're from real organizations
  • Urgent messages pressuring you to "act now" or verify your account
  • Links to fake websites that steal your login credentials
  • Requests for personal information via SMS or messaging apps
